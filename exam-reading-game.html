My Family and My Cat 🐈

My name is Tom. I am a boy. I have a mother and a sister.

We have a cat. The cat is in my room. It is on my chair.

On Sunday, I go to school by bus. My sister has a bag and a pencil.

In winter, I drink tea. My cat drinks water. I love my family!
